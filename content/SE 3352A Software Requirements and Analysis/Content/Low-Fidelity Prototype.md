A **low-fidelity prototype** (sketch / [[Wireframe]]) is a quick, rough drawing of a proposed solution, used to explore a business problem.

- Sketch **as much of the business as is needed** to understand the problem — not just a screen. Show the wider ecosystem.
- The more of the business you show, the better your chances of seeing (and solving) the **right** problem.

**Example — customs inspections of trucks at international borders:**
- *Sketch:* a truck approaches a scanner, its electronic cargo manifest is scanned and sent to the customs post, and a customs officer decides to wave it through or stop it for inspection.
- *Wireframe:* a screen showing the data tied to that candidate · Truck ID · Manifest ID · Origin · Destination · manifest contents · a **Stop and Inspect** button.
