A **storyboard** is a sequence of panels showing a candidate solution in use, scene by scene, with text under each panel describing what happens.

Borrowed from film: before shooting, the director and a storyboard artist draw every scene from the script — where the actors stand relative to the camera, the dialogue, and directions. The finished storyboard becomes the shooting plan for the whole movie.

**Example — candidate home security solution:**

| Panel | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Brad and Janet take the family away for the weekend. |
| 2 | The solution senses the car leaving, locks the doors, turns on the burglar alarm, activates the random-lights program and turns down the heating. |
| 3 | Before they return, Janet phones the solution to check the food supplies at home and asks it to thaw a frozen chicken. |
| 4 | The solution detects the car and its occupants, opens the garage door, unsets the alarm, plays their favourite homecoming music and starts cooking the chicken. |
